# 🍳 What Can I Cook?

**What Can I Cook?** is a simple, AI-powered recipe finder that helps you decide what to cook using the ingredients you already have.

Instead of searching through recipes and checking which ingredients you're missing, simply enter what you have in your kitchen and let the app suggest recipes you can make.

## ✨ Features

- 🥕 Enter ingredients you already have
- 🍳 Get recipe suggestions based on your ingredients
- ⏱️ Filter recipes by available cooking time
- 📋 View ingredients and cooking instructions
- 💾 Save ingredients locally
- 📱 Mobile-friendly and responsive
- 🤖 AI-powered recipe suggestions

## 🚀 How It Works

1. Enter the ingredients you have.
2. Choose how much time you have.
3. Click **Find Recipes**.
4. Get personalized recipe suggestions.
5. Choose a recipe and start cooking.

## 🎯 Goal

The goal is to make the everyday question **"What should I cook?"** easier to answer while helping people make use of the food they already have.

> No account. No complicated setup. Just enter your ingredients and cook. 🍳
